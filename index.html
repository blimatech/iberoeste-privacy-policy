Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 26, 2026

This policy explains what information we collect when you interact with our WhatsApp chatbot, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data.

1. Who We Are

This service is operated by M4I Lda. If you have any questions about this policy, you can contact us at geral@m4i.pt.

2. What Information We Collect

When you interact with our WhatsApp chatbot, we may collect:

  • Your WhatsApp phone number — used to identify your session and respond to you.
  • Messages you send — text, images, and location data shared during the conversation.
  • Location information — if you voluntarily share your location via WhatsApp, we store latitude, longitude, and location name to provide location-based services.
  • Contact details — name and contact type if provided during the conversation.
  • Images — photos you send are uploaded to secure cloud storage and linked to your session.
  • Session metadata — timestamps of your interactions, session state, and conversation history needed to maintain context.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your messages and navigate the conversation flow.
  • To provide AI-powered responses via OpenAI / ChatGPT for support queries.
  • To store session state so your conversation resumes correctly if interrupted.
  • To generate internal reports for service improvement (aggregated, not individually targeted).
  • To comply with applicable legal obligations.

4. How We Store Your Data

Your data is stored in Supabase, a secure cloud database hosted in the EU. Images are stored in Supabase Storage. Data is protected by industry-standard encryption at rest and in transit.

Session data is automatically cleaned up after a period of inactivity. Image files may be retained for a longer period for audit purposes.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal data. We share it only with:

  • Supabase — database and storage provider.
  • OpenAI — your messages may be processed by OpenAI's API to generate responses. See OpenAI's Privacy Policy.
  • Meta / WhatsApp — messages are delivered via the WhatsApp Business API. See WhatsApp's Privacy Policy.
  • Legal authorities if required by applicable law.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of the data we hold about you.
  • Rectification — ask us to correct inaccurate data.
  • Erasure — request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten").
  • Restriction — ask us to limit how we process your data.
  • Portability — receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at geral@m4i.pt.

7. Children's Privacy

This service is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the service after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

9. Contact Us

For any privacy-related questions or requests, please email us at geral@m4i.pt.
